Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Support Privacy Policy

DiscPop collects no data. It works entirely on your Mac.

What DiscPop does not do

  • It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.
  • It doesn't connect to the internet, and it has no permission to do so.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • It doesn't read the files on your discs or on any other drive.

What DiscPop uses

To show your drive and eject discs, DiscPop asks macOS for each optical drive's name and whether a disc is inserted, while the app is running. This information stays on your Mac, is never saved, and is gone when you quit the app.

DiscPop runs in Apple's App Sandbox, which limits it to what it needs to do this one job.

Apple

If you download DiscPop from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the purchase under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your personal information from Apple, apart from anything you choose to send us when you contact support.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you, and we don't share it with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
